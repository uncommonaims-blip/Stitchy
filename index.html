STITCHY — evolutionary journey · v3.1

Keyboard: A/D or arrows to move. Jump unlocks at 8-bit; flight and platforms at 16-bit; perspective at 32-bit. Touch: hold directional buttons. Collect glowing stitches; land on platforms from 16-bit onward. Flight energy recharges when you rest. Avoid red hazards. Damage costs stitches and can reverse evolution. Audio starts when enabled.
